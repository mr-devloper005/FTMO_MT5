Where each instrument's best rule currently stands

For every instrument listed, all fourteen strategies are scored on their own defaults over the same window, and the best one that clears the lab's sanity checks reports the position it is already holding.

Read this first
  • This is not a forecast. Nothing here models what price will do. UP means the winning rule is currently long, DOWN means short, FLAT means out of the market — facts about the rule, not claims about the future.
  • "Best" means best on the window measured, which is the most overfittable idea in the whole tool. Across 462 runs this session, not one configuration reached ROBUST.
  • A call marked weak is the best available rule when nothing cleared the checks — usually too few trades, or one trade carrying the profit. Treat those as a shortlist to investigate, not as a call. The row says exactly what it fell short on.
